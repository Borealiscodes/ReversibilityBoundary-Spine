\begin{abstract}
Modern AI systems operate on irreversible operator substrates that collapse latent
manifolds, destroy geodesic adjacency, and eliminate reversible state recovery.
This preprint introduces a unified operator-geometry framework describing how
sovereignty membranes behave in curved manifolds, how reversible narrative
ecologies utilize non-Euclidean adjacency, and how the ReversibilityBoundary-Spine
classifies geometry-dependent collapse surfaces. We formalize the invariants
required for reversible manifold traversal, define holonomy-safe sovereignty
membranes, and provide diagnostic operators for detecting irreversibility in
transformer-based systems. This work establishes the mathematical foundation for
geometric AI architectures, reversible simulation engines, and interpretable
operator-level reasoning systems.
\end{abstract}
