\section*{Operator Geometry Overview}

Let $(M, g)$ be a smooth manifold. An operator $U : M \to M$ evolves states
according to:
\[
x_{t+1} = U(x_t).
\]

Reversible operators satisfy:
\[
U^{-1}(U(x)) = x.
\]

Irreversible operators violate this condition, producing collapse surfaces where
latent manifold structure is destroyed.

We define the diagnostic vector:
\[
D(x) = (\kappa, \Delta H, \lambda, \epsilon_{\text{fp}}, \epsilon_{\text{inv}}),
\]
which measures curvature, holonomy deviation, Lyapunov growth, fixed-point
violation, and irreversibility error.
