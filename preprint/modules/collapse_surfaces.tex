\section*{Geometry-Dependent Collapse Surfaces}

Irreversible substrates exhibit collapse surfaces where:
\[
\epsilon_{\text{inv}} = \|U^{-1}(U(x)) - x\| > 0,
\]
\[
\Delta H > 0,
\]
\[
\lambda > 0.
\]

Curvature amplifies collapse:
\[
K > 0 \Rightarrow \Delta H \uparrow.
\]

Holonomy deviation predicts irreversibility:
\[
\Delta H > 0 \Rightarrow U \text{ is irreversible}.
\]

Hybrid substrates oscillate across the boundary:
\[
D(x_t) \text{ oscillates}.
\]
