\section*{Implications for AI Interpretability}

Irreversibility destroys:
\begin{itemize}
    \item latent manifold continuity,
    \item geodesic reasoning paths,
    \item holonomy-preserving memory,
    \item reversible state recovery.
\end{itemize}

Our operator-geometry framework provides a mathematically grounded method for
detecting collapse surfaces and drift accumulation in transformer systems.

This enables reversible AI architectures, geometric reasoning engines, and
interpretable operator-level systems.
