\section*{Sovereignty Membranes in Curved Manifolds}

A sovereignty membrane is an operator-invariant envelope $\mathcal{S}(x)$
satisfying:
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal{S}(x_t) = 0.
\]

Let $(M, g)$ have curvature $K$. We show:
\[
\nabla_K \mathcal{S}(x) = 0,
\]
meaning sovereignty membranes survive hyperbolic, spherical, warped, and
discontinuous geometries.

They preserve:
\begin{itemize}
    \item non-erasure invariants,
    \item continuity envelopes,
    \item holonomy-safe traversal,
    \item altitude sealing,
    \item reversible operator geometry.
\end{itemize}
